# 📖 Kindora User Guide

Welcome to Kindora! This guide helps you navigate the platform whether you are a Volunteer, Mentor, Organization, or System Administrator.

## 👥 Role Profiles & Navigation

Kindora uses role-based access to provide tailored experiences for each user type.

### 1. Volunteers
**Target Audience:** Individuals looking to donate their time and skills.
- **Dashboard (`/volunteer/dashboard`)**: View recommended opportunities and your upcoming shifts.
- **Opportunities (`/volunteer/opportunities`)**: Search for event-based or regular volunteering roles using advanced filters.
- **Applications**: Track the status of your applications (Pending, Approved, Rejected).
- **Profile (`/volunteer/profile`)**: Showcase your skills, availability, and past experience.

### 2. Mentors
**Target Audience:** Experienced professionals guiding volunteers or organizational staff.
- **Dashboard (`/mentor/dashboard`)**: Manage your assigned mentees and active programs.
- **Rosters**: View schedules and availability for mentorship sessions.
- **Messaging**: Integrated chat with your mentees.

### 3. Organizations
**Target Audience:** Non-profits and community groups hosting opportunities.
- **Dashboard (`/organization/dashboard`)**: An overview of active opportunities, total volunteers, and recent applications.
- **Opportunity Management**: Create and manage detailed listings for volunteer or mentor roles.
- **Applicant Tracking**: Review incoming applications, approve or reject them, and communicate with candidates.
- **Roster Management**: Schedule shifts and assign approved volunteers.

### 4. System Administrator
**Target Audience:** Internal staff managing the platform.
- **Dashboard (`/system-admin/dashboard`)**: Platform-wide analytics and system health.
- **User Management**: Overview of all registered users and organizations.
- **Access Control**: Note that System Admin accounts require an email ending in `@system.kindora.com`.

## 💬 Messaging & Notifications

- **Real-Time Chat**: Access your messages from the top navigation bar. You can chat directly with organizations, mentors, or volunteers you are connected with.
- **Notifications**: Check the bell icon for updates on applications, shift changes, and new messages.
